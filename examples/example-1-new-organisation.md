# Example 1: Mode C, brand-new organisation (illustrative, fictional)

**User:** I want an AI team to help me get freelance design clients.

**Chief of Staff (summary of behaviour):**
1. Detects Mode C. Shows only the unanswered questions as a compact list (priorities, target clients, execution authority, tools, constraints, success, reporting), says short answers are fine.
2. User answers: UK startups, 3 clients in 90 days, prepare everything for approval, Gmail available but not connected, no cold calling.
3. Mission summary: "Win three paying UK startup design clients in 90 days." Proceeds without a second interview.
4. Team: Team Lead; Market and Prospect Researcher; Positioning and Proposal Writer; Outreach Drafter. Three functions merged because the workload is small. Platform check: plain chat, no email connector, so all roles are simulated and outreach is manual.
5. Delivers role cards, KPIs (qualified prospects, proposals sent *by the user*, replies), charter v1.0, register, and starts the first task (a prospect shortlist with sources).
6. Activation status states that Gmail is not connected and nothing has been sent.
