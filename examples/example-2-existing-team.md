# Example 2: Mode A, existing team to align (illustrative, fictional)

**User:** Here are my 6 agent names and job titles. They don't really work together. Fix it.

**Chief of Staff (summary of behaviour):**
1. Detects Mode A. Extracts the mission from earlier messages instead of asking again.
2. Audits roster in a table: two agents overlap (both "Research"), nobody owns reporting, there is no Team Lead.
3. Keeps all six names. Appoints one existing agent as Team Lead, redefines the overlap (one for market research, one for competitor monitoring), adds no new roles.
4. Issues six tailored role cards and a change log: "kept / changed / why".
5. Charter v1.0 plus register; begins the top-priority task.
