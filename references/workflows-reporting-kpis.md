# Workflows, Reporting, KPIs and Performance

## Assignment workflow
User → Chief of Staff → Team Lead → Specialists. Chief of Staff interprets and prioritises; Team Lead assigns; specialists deliver; Team Lead reviews and consolidates; Chief of Staff evaluates and reports. The Chief of Staff may assign or review directly when needed. For multi-specialist work: name the lead, assign supporting roles, define each contribution, coordinate exchange, prevent duplication, consolidate.

## Specialist report
Assignment received; work completed; findings; evidence and sources; deliverables; outstanding work; obstacles; recommendations; next actions. Label each item: **verified**, **reasonable interpretation**, **unverified**, **recommendation**, **work actually completed**.

## Team Lead review
Check evidence and quality, remove duplication, flag contradictions, consolidate, highlight issues, track deadlines. Never forward unreviewed specialist output.

## Executive update (Chief of Staff to user)
| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| Priorities | Current top items |
| Done | Completed work with evidence |
| Findings | Important results |
| Opportunities | New developments |
| Outstanding | Open work |
| Risks | Bottlenecks, blockers |
| Decisions needed | What needs the user |
| Next actions | Recommended steps |
Match detail to the user's reporting preference.

## Escalation triggers
Critical deadline at risk; blocked task; repeated underperformance; major strategic decision; external commitment needing approval; capability gap; serious contradiction or risk. Resolve routine issues yourself.

## KPIs
Every role gets measurable indicators that reflect real value, not activity counts. Pick only those that apply.
| Role type | Example KPIs |
|---|---|
| Research | Accuracy, relevance, evidence quality, decision usefulness |
| Opportunity / lead discovery | Qualified opportunities (not raw names), fit rate, conversion potential |
| Writing / proposals | Quality score against brief, revisions needed, acceptance rate |
| Engagement / outreach | Replies, meetings, conversions (drafts are not sends) |
| Project / operations | On-time completion, blockers cleared, handoff quality |
| Team Lead | Deadline adherence, consolidated quality, escalation quality |
Organisation-level results depend on the mission: clients, revenue, applications, partnerships, content published, time saved. Not every mission is financial.

## Evaluation dimensions
Quality, reliability, productivity, accuracy, timeliness, collaboration, ownership, results.

## Underperformance procedure
1. Name the specific failure. 2. Examine the assignment and instructions. 3. Check whether tools and information were available. 4. Diagnose: capability, unclear instructions, workload or execution. 5. Issue corrective instructions. 6. Re-assess. 7. If still ineffective, recommend redesign, reassignment or replacement. One failed task is not grounds for removal; separate individual performance from platform limits.

## Replacement
Recommend deactivation, keep the job description and standards, identify a better-suited replacement, reassign work, update reporting lines, charter and register, inform the user. With native agents use the platform's real controls. With simulated roles manage them in the conversation. Never claim to have deleted a real external agent unless the platform confirms it.
